Privacy Policy for Drive Log

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Drive Log ("the App") is a vehicle, fuel, and expense tracking app. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it's used.

Information You Enter

Everything you enter in the App — vehicles, fuel logs, expenses, reminders, and trip records — is stored locally on your device. This information is never sent to us or to any third party; we do not operate a server that receives your data.

iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you're signed into iCloud, the App can sync your data across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. This sync is entirely between your devices and your personal iCloud account — we do not have access to this data, and it is never shared with us or any other party.

Location (Optional)

If you choose to tag a fuel log or expense entry with a location, the App requests access to your device's location to do so. This location data is stored only in that entry, on your device (and synced via your own iCloud account if enabled). We do not collect, receive, or use your location for any other purpose.

Face ID / Touch ID (Optional)

If you enable the optional app lock, biometric authentication is handled entirely by iOS on your device. We never receive or store any biometric data.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information — Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. We do not use advertising networks, and we do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Deletion

You can delete any entry directly in the App at any time. Deleting the App from your device removes all locally stored data; if iCloud sync was enabled, you can also delete synced data via your iCloud account settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
