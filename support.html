Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync data across my devices?

Drive Log syncs automatically via iCloud when you're signed in on multiple devices with the same Apple ID. You can check sync status under Settings → Backup & Restore.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Backup & Restore → Create Backup to export a file with all your vehicles, logs, and reminders, which you can restore later or on another device.

What's included with Premium?

DriveLog Premium unlocks unlimited vehicles, advanced analytics, custom themes, advanced maintenance schedules, receipts & documents, and reports & export. See Settings → DriveLog Premium for details.
